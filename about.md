I’m Indivar, a software engineer working with Python and exploring AI. I build tools, make small games, and contribute to open source, including [CycloneDX Python Library](https://github.com/CycloneDX/cyclonedx-python-lib/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3AindiVar0508) and [SQLAlchemy](https://github.com/sqlalchemy/sqlalchemy/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3AindiVar0508).

My work also includes a co-authored IEEE paper on decision tree algorithms, as well as a book chapter on regression algorithms written with my professor. You can find both in my [publications]({{ '/work/#publications' | relative_url }}).

I’m from Uttar Pradesh and have lived across India. I enjoy learning, finding an explanation that makes an idea click, and exploring questions about deep space. This site is where I share my projects and the things I work out along the way.

Water conservation and social work for education matter to me. I also co-organize [BangPypers](https://bangalore.python.org.in/), a Python community in Bengaluru.
